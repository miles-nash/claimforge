## Cycle 0: Setup And Target Selection

Created the 30-minute heartbeat automation: `frontier-push-loop`.

Initial research points toward autonomous scientific verification rather than autonomous paper generation.

Evidence:

- AI Scientist and AI Scientist-v2 show end-to-end research automation is becoming real, but this increases the value of independent verification.
- CORE-Bench frames computational reproducibility as a real-world benchmark, with the best reported agent only reaching 21% accuracy on the hardest task in the abstract.
- PaperBench evaluates full paper replication and reports a 21.0% average replication score for the best tested agent in OpenAI's April 2025 release.
- FIRE-Bench reports low current performance on full-cycle research rediscovery and identifies planning as a major failure source.
- Reproducible LM evaluation work emphasizes transparency and setup sensitivity as core evaluation problems.

Decision:

Target the narrow field of Codex-native research verification: planning and execution workflows for reproducing/falsifying computational claims.

2. Choose the smallest runnable task family.
3. Build a local evidence ledger template.
4. Request missing access from Miles only when a concrete run is blocked.
